# Research demonstration

Use the original local service at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, or the explicitly isolated release test service documented in the final status. Neither is a public cloud URL.

1. Confirm the interface reports search readiness and 307 archived IP India documents.
2. Search `Patents Act section 3 traditional knowledge invention exclusion`.
3. Read a returned passage, expand it and inspect its provenance panel. Verify the source SHA-256, document and chunk IDs, capture date and available section/page anchors.
4. Open the official-source link in a separate tab and read the surrounding provision. A link being live does not establish current force.
5. Try `Patents Act section 64 revocation`, `Patent Rules fees` or `Designs Act registration`. Filters are optional and search ranking is not a legal applicability decision.
6. Open source status and confirm unsupported India Code/TKDL coverage and the unknown legal-status notice. Biodiversity/ABS evidence is not certified for this release.
7. Repeat at a mobile viewport, including results, citation details and error/retry controls.

The public UI provides research extracts and source exploration. It does not provide generated legal conclusions or confidential-invention intake. Do not submit personal information, unpublished inventions or other confidential material.

The screenshots included with the submission are labelled with their actual local scope. Synthetic browser fixtures are separately identified and cannot count as real corpus or cloud tests. A future cloud demo requires replacing this local workflow with a real HTTPS domain and rerunning the external smoke/browser tests.
