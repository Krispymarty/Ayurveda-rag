RESEARCH, WITH CARE

A little context goes a long way.

How should I search?

Begin with an instrument and a topic, such as “Patents Act section 3 traditional knowledge”. Broaden the wording if no passage matches. Expand each passage, inspect its section/page reference and open the official document to read surrounding provisions.

What does a result establish?

A result identifies text in an archived official source. Search ranking does not determine whether a rule applies to your situation, is complete, or remains in force. Verify amendments, commencement and current status with authoritative records and an appropriately qualified adviser.

Privacy and research limitations

Search terms are sent to the service to retrieve passages. Evidence search alone does not invoke an answer model. The classroom demo sends questions and evidence to Groq or Google Gemini only with your consent. Do not submit sensitive personal information, unpublished inventions or confidential material. Access may be limited to protect availability. AyurIP provides legal information for research, not legal or medical advice.
